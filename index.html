01 / THE IDEA

Less to keep
in your head.
More in sync.

Family care involves many small details. PISVA helps bring those details together so everyone can understand what needs attention and who is responsible.

From a time-sensitive follow-up to an upcoming appointment, the app gives family members a clearer view of shared responsibilities — while keeping ownership explicit.

✳
Clarity creates confidence.Responsibilities are visible, handoffs require acceptance, and private documents remain separate from simple links or notes.
